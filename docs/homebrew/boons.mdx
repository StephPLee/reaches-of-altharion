# Boons

Boons stand as the ultimate testament to a character's supremacy. Upon reaching level 20, every character is granted a single boon, marking the start of their ascension to true greatness. If a character finds and defeats a legendary World Boss, they gain an additional boon of their choice, up to 2 extra boons from bosses. Boons may only be taken once unless they specify otherwise.

Every time a level 20 character receives a boon, they may increase one of their statistics, and its maximum, by 2, up to a maximum of 30. The level 19 monoclass boon does not grant this stat increase. All selected boons and their stat increases must be logged [here](https://discord.com/channels/1417076658460033077/1452988342038822922).
